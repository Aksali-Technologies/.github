# Security policy

This policy covers every repository in the [Aksali Technologies](https://github.com/Aksali-Technologies) organisation, and the website at [aksali.com](https://aksali.com), unless a repository has its own.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues **privately**, never in a public issue, discussion or pull request.

- **Email:** [hello@aksali.com](mailto:hello@aksali.com), with **"Security"** at the start of the subject line
- **Include:** what you found, where, steps to reproduce, and the impact you believe it has

We aim to acknowledge a report within three working days, keep you informed while we investigate, and tell you when it is fixed. With your permission, we are glad to credit you.

## Testing responsibly

Please do not access, change or delete data that is not yours, run denial-of-service or load tests, or degrade a service for others. Act in good faith and give us reasonable time to fix an issue before telling anyone else, and we will not pursue legal action against you for research done within this policy.
