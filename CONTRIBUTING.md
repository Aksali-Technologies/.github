# Contributing to Aksali Technologies repositories

Thank you for your interest. Most Aksali repositories are worked on by the Aksali team and people we invite; a repository's own `CONTRIBUTING.md`, where it has one, takes precedence over this file.

## Reporting a problem

- **Security issues:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report privately by email.
- **Anything else:** open an issue in the repository concerned, or tell us through [aksali.com/contact](https://aksali.com/contact).

## How we work

- **One change per commit,** with a message in the imperative that says what it does and why.
- **Every change goes through a pull request,** with its checks passing and a review before it is merged.
- **Only true, verifiable claims** in any content, and results reported as they were measured, limits included.
- **No secrets** in any repository: keys and credentials live in the hosting provider's environment settings.

Everyone taking part follows the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
