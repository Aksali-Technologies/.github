# Code of conduct

Aksali Technologies holds its work to one standard, and the way we work together to the same one.

## What we expect

- **Respect.** Treat everyone with courtesy, whatever their background, identity, experience or role.
- **Straight answers, kindly given.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Give feedback that is specific and useful, and take it in the same spirit.
- **Honesty.** Say what you know and what you do not. Report results as they are, including the ones that complicate the story.
- **Care for the work and for others' time.** Keep changes clear, explain your reasoning, and follow through on what you take on.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal or political attacks
- Unwelcome attention, or sexual language and imagery
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in this repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere you represent Aksali Technologies.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, email [hello@aksali.com](mailto:hello@aksali.com) with **"Conduct"** in the subject. Reports are handled promptly and in confidence. Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behaviour is expected to stop at once. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may remove anyone from the project for behaviour that breaks this code.
